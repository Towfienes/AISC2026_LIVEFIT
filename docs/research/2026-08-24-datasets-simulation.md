# TOPIC 5 — Datasets & Simulation for LiveLift Validation

## (a) KuaiLive — VERIFIED, use it (primary offline benchmark + calibration source)

- **Status**: Real and downloadable. Zenodo record 16565801, v1 published 2025-07-29, last modified 2026-04-23; paper accepted at **SIGIR 2026** ("KuaiLive: A Real-time Interactive Dataset for Live Streaming Recommendation", arXiv:2508.05633). ([Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/16565801), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05633), [project page](https://imgkkk574.github.io/KuaiLive/))
- **Download mechanics**: single file `KuaiLive.zip`, **858.2 MB** (MD5 `9f0f13950f677a0d9d2224c3e7abb553`): `wget https://zenodo.org/record/16565801/files/KuaiLive.zip && unzip KuaiLive.zip`. No registration/agreement gate.
- **Size/coverage**: 23,772 users, 452,621 streamers, 11,613,708 live rooms, 5,357,998 interactions over **May 5–25, 2025** (21 days): 4,909,515 clicks, 196,526 comments, 179,311 likes, 72,646 gifts.
- **Schema (verified from paper/site)**: four interaction tables (click / comment / like / gift; each with user_id, streamer/room id, timestamps, and behavior details such as watch duration and gift price); **user features** (20 cols: gender, age, country, device_brand, device_price, reg_timestamp, fans_num, follow_num, is_video_author, first_watch_live_timestamp, accu_watch_live_cnt/duration, is_live_author + 7 encrypted vectors); **streamer features** (23 cols incl. live_operation_tag, accu_live_cnt/duration, accu_play_cnt/duration + 7 encrypted vectors); **room table** (start_timestamp, end_timestamp, live_type, live_content_category, live_name_representation). Includes exposed-but-not-clicked impressions, so per-room CTR is computable — exactly LiveLift's primary outcome shape.
- **License caveat**: project page/paper say **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**; the Zenodo metadata shows CC BY 4.0. Treat it as **NC** (fine for AISC'26 student use; do not ship inside a commercial LiveLift build).
- **What to do**: (1) compute empirical distributions — room-level CTR, dwell time, arrival intensity within a live session, comment/click ratios — and use them as **simulator calibration targets**; (2) run your outer/inner-tier estimators "offline replay"-style on real click sequences as a realism check; (3) benchmark PhoBERT-adjacent intent signals against comment-to-click correlations (comments are Chinese, so use only rates/timing, not text).

## (b) LSEC (KDD 2021) — VERIFIED but SKIP for causal work

- Paper: "Leveraging Tripartite Interaction Information from Live Stream E-Commerce for Improving Product Recommendation", KDD 2021, JD Technology data ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3447548.3467151), [arXiv:2106.03415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03415)).
- Only **LSEC-Small** is released, via GitHub Releases as 4 split archives (`LSEC-Small-aa..ad.dummy.gz`), reassembled with `cat LSEC-Small-* | tar -xzvf -` ([repo](https://github.com/yusanshi/LSEC-GNN)). The repo documents it as a **tripartite user–streamer–product graph** (buy/view edges); column schema, timestamps, and license are **not documented** in the README — you must inspect the metadata dir after extraction.
- **Verdict: skip.** No documented timestamps → useless for switchback/time-block validation; no license → risky to redistribute derived stats. At most, mine it for user–streamer–product degree distributions (popularity skew priors for the simulator).

## (c) KuaiLive-M3 — real, but SKIP

- "KuaiLive-M3: A Multi-Modal, Multi-Domain, and Multi-Feedback Dataset" ([arXiv:2607.24862](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24862v1), Jul 2026; site: imgkkk574.github.io/KuaiLive-M3). 21,938 users, ~35M live-streaming + 111M short-video interactions, ~88M segment-level multimodal embeddings, 25,403 questionnaire responses; CC BY 4.0.
- **Verdict: skip** for LiveLift's core validation — the multimodal segment embeddings and cross-domain scope add hundreds of GB of complexity you don't need for block-level CTR causal inference. Revisit only if you later model "what the streamer is saying now" as a covariate.

## (e) Simulator: best practice + concrete design

**Why simulate**: your ~650 real blocks can't tell you whether your estimator is biased — only synthetic data with **known injected effects** can (this is the "calibrated simulation" principle: anchor the generator to your empirical distribution, then dial in ground-truth effect and confounding — see *Credence*, "Validating Causal Inference Methods", ICML 2022, [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/parikh22a.html)). The switchback theory to validate against is Bojinov–Simchi-Levi–Zhao, "Design and Analysis of Switchback Experiments", *Management Science* 2023 ([arXiv:2009.00148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00148)): order-*p* carryover, minimax-optimal block lengths, randomization-based inference — implement their Horvitz–Thompson estimator with burn-in as one of your candidates. Prior-art simulators: **RecoGym** (Criteo, [github](https://github.com/criteo-research/reco-gym)), **Open Bandit Pipeline** ([zr-obp](https://github.com/st-tech/zr-obp)) — reuse OBP's IPS/DR/SNIPS implementations for the inner tier rather than rewriting them — **KuaiSim** ([arXiv:2309.12645](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.12645)), Virtual-Taobao (AAAI 2019), RecSim. None model livestream sessions, so build a small custom discrete-event simulator (~500 lines Python) and validate estimators through OBP.

**LiveLift session simulator sketch** (`simulator/` module, 1-second event clock, 90-min sessions):

1. **Arrivals**: nonhomogeneous Poisson with baseline λ(t) = ramp-up → plateau → decay (shape fit to LiveRec presence data; scale fit to your Live Lab weeks 1–2), plus a **Hawkes self-excitation** term where pin-switch/flash-deal events add a decaying intensity bump (kernel φ·e^(−t/τ_h)) — Hawkes is the standard model for attention bursts (Hawkes Intensity Processes, [arXiv:1602.06033](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.06033)).
2. **Departures**: per-viewer exponential hazard h(t) = h₀·exp(boredom(t)), boredom rising when the pinned product's affinity for that viewer is low → dwell times and audience composition react to treatment (realistic interference channel).
3. **Clicks**: each viewer has latent vector u (drawn from KuaiRec-derived clusters); pinned product j gives per-minute click prob σ(α + u·pⱼ + β_pin + s(t)); calibrate α so block CTR matches KuaiLive room CTR and your Live Lab pilot.
4. **Treatment injection (ground truth)**: ON blocks apply your policy; OFF blocks a fixed heuristic (rotate by inventory). Inject effect as additive logit lift δ on σ(·) when the policy's pin differs from the heuristic's; you can make δ heterogeneous in t and product to test CATE. True block-level effect is computed by simulating both counterfactual arms with common random numbers (same arrival stream, same u draws) — this gives exact per-block ground truth, not just an average.
5. **Carryover (order-p)**: three mechanisms, each with a knob — (i) *audience persistence*: viewers recruited during an ON block stay ~geometric(φ_a) blocks; (ii) *demand cannibalization*: a viewer who clicked has click hazard suppressed ×(1−κ) for m minutes; (iii) *hype decay*: the Hawkes bump crosses block boundaries. Sweep φ_a, κ, τ_h from 0 (no carryover) upward; verify the Bojinov et al. result that misspecifying p slightly upward is safe at your n.
6. **Validation protocol**: (A/A) 1,000 null sessions → check false-positive rate ≈ α for every estimator; then power curves over δ ∈ {0.02…0.15 logit}, block length ∈ {5, 10, 15 min}, carryover on/off, at n = 650 blocks → pick block length and burn-in rule, and report the **MDE** in the final paper. Also replay the inner tier through OBP's DR estimator using your logged propensities to confirm unbiasedness before the Live Lab starts.

**Bottom line**: KuaiLive (download now, 858 MB) + LiveRec-100k + KuaiRec cover calibration; skip LSEC and KuaiLive-M3; build the custom Hawkes/NHPP session simulator with common-random-number counterfactuals and the six-knob carryover design above, and make the simulator study a headline section of the AISC'26 submission — it's what converts "we ran a shop" into "our estimator is provably calibrated."
